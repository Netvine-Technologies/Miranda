1. Nail It Academy (United Kingdom)
To: info@nailit-academy.co.uk
Subject: Partnership idea for Nail It Academy graduates
Course evidence: https://nailit-academy.co.uk/pages/in-academy-beginners-gel-tech-course

A partnership for your graduates

Hi there,

I’m Shane from Booking Tree, and I wanted to share a partnership idea for students completing your nail technician courses.

As your graduates start taking on clients, Booking Tree can help them accept online bookings, collect payments and send appointment reminders.

The idea is simple: recommend Booking Tree to your students when they finish their course, and earn 20% of each referred student’s subscription for their first 12 months, while they remain a paying customer.

There’s no cost to take part, and we would provide a dedicated referral link so you receive credit for the students you introduce.

Would you be interested in seeing how this could work for Nail It Academy?

View the programme and enquire: https://bookingtree.io/partners/educators#partner-enquiry

Thanks,
Shane
Booking Tree

If this isn’t relevant, just let me know and I won’t follow up.
